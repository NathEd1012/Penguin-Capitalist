========================================================================
SPREAD CALCULATION FORMULA
========================================================================

Base spread = max(
    mid_price * 0.0002,           # 0.02% of price
    (high - low) * 0.05           # 5% of candle range
)

Market hours adjustment:
  - First 15 min after open:  spread *= 1.5 (50% wider)
  - Last 15 min before close: spread *= 1.2 (20% wider)
  - Regular hours:            no adjustment

Prices:
  bid = mid_price - spread/2
  ask = mid_price + spread/2


========================================================================
KEY PARAMETERS
========================================================================

Parameter                      Default      Use Case
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
base_price_factor              0.0002       % of price
                                            Lower = more liquid market

volatility_factor              0.05         × (high - low)
                                            Higher = more spread sensitivity

market_open_time               "14:30"      UTC (9:30 AM ET)
market_close_time              "20:00"      UTC (4:00 PM ET)

opening_spread_multiplier      1.5          1.5 = 50% wider at open
closing_spread_multiplier      1.2          1.2 = 20% wider at close

opening_period_minutes         15           Minutes after open
closing_period_minutes         15           Minutes before close
